Costa Brava destination wedding: how to coordinate music and technical production remotely

Planning a Costa Brava wedding from another city or country means music and technical production should be clear before you reach the venue. A shared plan avoids last-minute choices and gives the ceremony, cocktail hour, dinner and party a natural flow.

Share a simple running order early

Arrival, ceremony, speeches, cocktail hour and dancing determine what each moment needs. An early running order lets the planner, venue and technical team coordinate soundchecks, access and room changes.

Our event production in Girona brings that coordination together across Costa Brava, Girona and Barcelona.

Choose music for the whole day

Destination weddings often bring together guests from different countries and generations. Sharing references, must-play songs and boundaries guides the DJ while leaving room to respond to the real atmosphere.

A wedding DJ in Costa Brava can connect every part of the celebration without turning it into a rigid playlist.

Confirm the technical plan with the venue

Power, load-in, sound limits, rain and outdoor areas should be reviewed before setup. A technical visit or video call with photographs and a floor plan makes it possible to plan the right equipment.

Blend live music and DJ without breaking the flow

Live musicians can add character to the ceremony or cocktail hour, while a DJ takes over for the party. Explore our music for ceremonies and cocktail hours to create one coherent experience.
