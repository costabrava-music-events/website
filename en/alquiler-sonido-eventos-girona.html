Sound and lighting for events in Girona

We install sound and lighting systems for weddings, private events, corporate events, openings and parties in Girona and Costa Brava. We work with external DJs, live music, speeches, ceremonies, cocktails and indoor or outdoor parties.

What the technical setup includes

  • Main PA system, monitors or delays when the space requires it.
  • Mixer, cabling, stands, microphones and connections for DJ or musicians.
  • Soundcheck and level adjustment before guests arrive.
  • Lighting for dance floor, dinner, facade, garden or transition areas.
  • Delivery, installation, technical assistance and dismantling.

Information we need

  1. Date, location, schedule and approximate guest count.
  2. Event format: ceremony, cocktail, dinner, speeches, party or multiple zones.
  3. Whether the DJ brings their own setup or needs booth equipment, players or controller.
  4. Technical rider from artists, external DJ or specific venue requirements.

FAQs

Can you work with an external DJ?

Yes. We only need their rider or confirmation of the booth equipment required.

Is the technician optional?

For events with sound and lighting we recommend an on-site technician to control levels, changes and incidents.

Do you include wireless microphones?

Yes, we can include handheld or lapel microphones depending on speeches, ceremony or presentations.

Do you cover outdoor events?

Yes, provided there is safe power, venue permission and proper technical planning.
