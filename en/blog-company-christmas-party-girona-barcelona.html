Company Christmas party in Girona, Costa Brava and Barcelona: from dinner to the dance floor

Dinner finishes, coffee arrives and some colleagues start reaching for their coats. That moment decides whether the celebration ends there or turns into a party. When planning a company Christmas party, music and production need to prepare that transition from the start.

Design the evening around the team

A company dinner brings together people with different musical tastes and ways of celebrating. Some want to dance, others prefer conversation, and some are looking forward to meeting colleagues from another office. The aim is to make room for everyone while keeping the evening moving.

Before choosing songs, decide on the format: lunch with music afterwards, a drinks reception with a live act, or dinner followed by a DJ. In a Girona restaurant, a Barcelona hotel or a Costa Brava masia, the venue and closing time shape the proposal as much as the number of guests.

A Christmas party DJ needs a useful brief

A DJ for a company Christmas party needs more than a list of hits. Share approximate age ranges, whether international teams are attending, favourite styles and songs to avoid. Disco, pop, 80s and 90s classics or current tracks can provide a starting point; the order and mixing should respond to the dance floor.

Choose a handful of must-play songs and leave room to read the audience. Playing every request in order can break the flow. If there are awards, a toast or a surprise, prepare music cues and pauses in advance. Explore our approach to DJs for private parties in Costa Brava and apply it to your team celebration.

Live music for arrival and conversation

A live performance during drinks gives the celebration a personality before dancing begins. Pop-rock covers, rumba, boleros or chilled music can suit different atmospheres. Choose the duration, repertoire and volume for that part of the evening rather than asking a band to do the same job all night.

When combining a band and DJ, decide where each will set up and how the handover will work. A dismantling break at the liveliest point can cool the party down. Look at live bands for celebrations and agree a prepared transition with the technical team.

Sound for dinner, speeches and dancing

During dinner, guests should be able to talk without competing with speakers. For a management message or team recognition, the priority changes: speech needs to reach the tables at the back clearly. The microphone, speaker placement and an advance sound check matter more than turning everything up at the last minute.

The dance floor needs its own coverage. If dinner and dancing are in different rooms, allow for two sound zones and the route between them. When considering event sound hire in Girona, confirm what the setup includes, who operates the microphones and how speeches will be coordinated with catering.

Christmas lighting that works for the room

A brightly lit room can feel like a work meeting; a room that is too dark makes conversation and movement difficult. Combine warm lighting for tables, ambient light in selected areas and a different lighting scene for dancing.

Company colours can appear on walls or venue features without colouring the whole dinner. Check reflections, photography, access routes and the DJ booth location before agreeing the setup. Existing Christmas decorations are part of the overall picture too: every corner does not need an effect.

What to check with the venue before booking

Ask whether the room is exclusive, when music must finish and what conditions apply to outside equipment. Check loading access, setup time, electrical supply and venue restrictions. An installed system may work for background music without meeting the needs of a party or live band.

For Costa Brava terraces or outdoor areas, agree an indoor alternative with the venue. If colleagues are travelling from different towns, coordinate transport times with the end of the session. These decisions help size the technical event production and prevent booking equipment that cannot be used.

How to request quotes you can compare

Send the date, town, venue, expected guest count and timing for each stage. Specify whether you need a DJ, band, speech microphones, sound in several zones and lighting. Ask proposals to distinguish performance time, setup and dismantling, technical support and conditions for extending the schedule.

The price depends on the whole event, not just the hours of music. Comparing offers with different coverage can lead to the wrong decision. October is a useful time to define your format and check availability for November and December, without assuming every date will be free.

Company Christmas party questions

DJ or live music? A DJ provides a flexible dance session, while live music creates a performance moment. Both can be combined when the schedule and venue allow.

How long should the party last? Work backwards from the venue closing time, the actual dinner finish and any planned speeches. Give dancing a continuous block rather than interrupting it with announcements.
