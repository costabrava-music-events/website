Technical plan B for outdoor weddings in Costa Brava

How to prepare a technical plan B for an outdoor wedding in Costa Brava: sound, lighting, power and timing without improvisation.

Plan B does not start when it rains

At an outdoor wedding, the risk is not only rain. Wind, humidity, direct sun, area changes, difficult access and timing delays also matter. If plan B is discussed on the day, it is already late.

The sensible approach is to define in advance which parts of the event can move, which equipment needs protection and what decisions will be made based on the forecast and the actual venue conditions.

Sound and power: two areas that cannot be improvised

The ceremony, cocktail hour and party rarely need the same system. In some venues it is better to work by areas instead of relying on one setup. Power capacity, cable runs, outlets and the existence of a covered alternative with reasonable access should also be checked.

Lighting that is useful, not only beautiful

Outdoor wedding lighting should not be designed only for photos. Entrances, pathways, bars, dinner areas and dance floor all need clear spatial definition. When conditions change, strong lighting also helps redirect guest flow and preserve the atmosphere.

If the event ends in a garden, masia or terrace, our ambient lighting for weddings in Costa Brava service gives a more detailed view.

Music, suppliers and fast decisions

Plan B should include who decides to move the ceremony, how much time the team needs to reconfigure and how that change is communicated to the venue, planner, musicians or DJ. That coordination matters as much as the equipment itself.
