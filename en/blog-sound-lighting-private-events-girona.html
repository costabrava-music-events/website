Sound and lighting for private events in Girona: how to size the setup

At a private event, sound and lighting are not just technical extras. They directly shape the experience. A dinner can feel elegant or cold. A dance floor can fill up or lose momentum. A speech can move people or disappear if the microphone setup is not right.

Start by understanding the event journey

The question is not only how many guests will attend. It also matters where they will be at each moment. An event may include an outdoor welcome, a garden cocktail hour, dinner under a marquee and an indoor party. Each area needs a different solution.

A professional setup is designed by scenes: arrival, speeches, background music, dinner, dance floor and closing.

Wireless microphones for speeches and presentations

At private events with speeches, anniversaries, launches or family celebrations, microphones are essential. A handheld microphone can be enough when only a few people speak. A lapel microphone can work better when speakers need freedom of movement.

The system must be stable, cover the right area and be tested before the event begins. It should not depend on a last-minute check.

Distributed sound for cocktails and dinners

When guests are spread across a terrace, garden or large room, it is often better to use several sound points at moderate volume than a few very loud speakers. The result is more comfortable and allows conversation without losing atmosphere.

This is especially important in masias, restaurants, hotels and venues with different levels or open areas.

Ambient lighting and party lighting

Not all lighting has the same purpose. Ambient lighting dresses the space and creates warmth. Dance-floor lighting works with energy: colour, movement, intensity and rhythm.

Many private events benefit from both layers: an elegant one for reception and dinner, and a more dynamic one for the final party.

The technician is part of the service

A properly sized setup needs supervision. Timings, volume, positions or microphone needs can change during the event. Having a technician on site allows the team to react without the client or guests having to manage anything.
