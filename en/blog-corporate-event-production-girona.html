Technical production for corporate events in Girona: sound, lighting and music without improvisation

How to coordinate sound, lighting, music and suppliers so a corporate event in Girona runs without improvisation.

A corporate event needs more than equipment

For an opening, presentation, company dinner or brand activation, the technical side should not be reduced to placing speakers. The goal is for the message to be heard, the venue to feel intentional and the transitions to feel natural.

That is why it helps to think in terms of technical production: sound, lighting, music, microphones, setup timings and coordination with the venue.

A technical briefing before quoting

Before defining equipment, it is important to understand the format, guest count, event zones, schedule, speeches, brand needs and whether there will be a DJ, live music or audiovisual content.

That information prevents overbuilding or undersizing the setup. It also clarifies power, access, load-in, soundcheck and dismantling timings.

Clear sound for speeches and atmosphere

Corporate events usually have two different needs: speech and atmosphere. Speech requires stable microphones, even coverage and level control. Music needs presence without covering conversations.

If the event uses multiple areas, distributed sound often works better than a few speakers running too loud.

Lighting with brand intention

Lighting can completely change how a space is perceived. A facade, garden, room or photocall area can gain presence with a considered lighting proposal.

The goal is not simply more light, but lighting with purpose: entrances, walkways, stage, bars, dinner and dance floor if the event ends with a party.

Coordination on the event day

A technician or team coordinator can handle schedule changes, volume adjustments, microphone checks, music cues or venue needs without placing that responsibility on the client.
