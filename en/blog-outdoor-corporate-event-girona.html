Outdoor corporate event in Girona: how to plan sound, lighting and music that keeps networking flowing

A terrace, garden or masia near Girona can make a corporate event feel less formal. But being outdoors does not solve the technical side: presentations must be heard, conversations need room and music should support the experience without competing with it.

Define what needs to happen in every part of the event

A team day, brand presentation and client cocktail need different energy. Separating arrival, content, break, networking and closing helps decide where the stage belongs, which areas need speech coverage and when music can become more present.

This sequence avoids one oversized system and makes it possible to build event production in Girona around the brand's actual objectives.

Outdoors, speech is designed around coverage, not volume

Wind, guest distance and ambient noise all change perception. For speeches, panels or an awards moment, it is more effective to cover the listening area properly, choose the right microphones and schedule a soundcheck than simply turn the system up.

A sound and lighting setup for events in Girona can combine reinforcement, microphones and technical crew for the venue and guest count.

Lighting also gives the event its structure

As daylight fades, light should show guests where to enter, where to talk and where each moment is happening. Lighting access points, routes and a facade or planting improves the experience without overproducing the event.

For a cocktail or dinner, keep tables warm and reserve a more dynamic layer for the musical close.

Music should support networking

On arrival and during conversation, a controlled-volume selection creates rhythm without asking guests to raise their voices. Later, a DJ or live format can build naturally if the programme allows and the audience responds.

Our music for private events in Girona also adapts to corporate celebrations that need a considered sonic identity.

Plan B: power, timings and rain

A well-produced outdoor event accounts for power points, equipment protection, sound limits, load-in access and an alternative if weather changes. Confirming these with the venue and suppliers before load-in prevents improvised decisions once guests arrive.
