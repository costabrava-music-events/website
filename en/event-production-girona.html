What we can coordinate

  • Music direction and selection of artists, DJs or live formats.

When full production makes sense

  1. When the event uses several zones with different technical needs.
  2. When there is an external DJ, live music, speeches and party on the same day.
  3. When the venue does not provide enough sound, lighting or technical coordination.
  4. When you want a premium proposal without relying on isolated suppliers.
