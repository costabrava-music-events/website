How we work

  1. We collect details about the venue, guest count, timings and desired style.
  2. We propose the right music format and technical setup for the event.
  3. We coordinate power, access, setup times and sound points.
  4. We run the event with musical judgement and technical control.

FAQs

Do you only work in Girona?

We are based around Costa Brava and Girona, but we can review events in other areas of Catalonia.

Can you provide only DJ or only sound?

Yes. We can handle the full music side or only the technical setup if you already have an artist or DJ.

Is lighting quoted separately?

We adapt it to the space and objective: warm ambience, dance floor, outdoor areas or a more scenic setup.

Do you need to visit the venue?

For medium or complex events it is recommended. Otherwise, we can work with plans, photos and venue contact details.

Need a proposal for your private event?

We prepare a clear proposal based on venue, guests, timings, music and lighting.
